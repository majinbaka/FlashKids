## Context

What forces this decision: the requirement, the constraint, and what is true in
the repository today. State facts with `path/to/file.dart:42` references, and
say plainly what is proposed versus what exists.

## Decision

What is decided, in the imperative. Be specific enough that a reviewer can tell
whether a given change complies. Number the clauses if there are several.

## Consequences

What becomes easier, what becomes harder, and what is now forbidden. Name the
rules this creates for future changes and where they are enforced — a skill, a
test, CI, or review only.

## Scope

What this record does **not** decide, so the next change does not read more into
it than was agreed.

## Alternatives considered

Each alternative and the specific reason it was not chosen. One or two sentences
each; this is the part that stops the decision from being re-litigated.
